    How to play

    Each battle lasts five turns. Your energy equals the current turn number.

    Play any number of cards you can afford, then end your turn.

    The opponent plays automatically. Cards remain on the board until destroyed.

    After turn five, the side with the highest total power wins.

    There is no maximum hand size.
